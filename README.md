# One Star, Five Stars for the Food — entry

A tiny Python program that writes one sentence in the voice of an alien
tourist reviewing planet Earth to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as the
bounty contract requires (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, nothing else).

The line:

> One star: gravity is aggressive, the weather is personal, and the natives photograph our fleet instead of fleeing. Five stars for the dumplings: we came to conquer, we stayed for seconds.

It was picked in an in-session tournament of six candidates against the
judge's published criteria: the funniest, most convincingly alien sentence
that lands both halves of the brief — a genuine one-star complaint and a
real five-star compliment about the food. The invasion that gives up and
asks for seconds is the turn the whole sentence is built around.
